Understanding LLM Attacks and SOC Utilization
LLM Attacks: Large Language Models (LLMs) are vulnerable to attacks that exploit their input-output behavior, such as manipulating prompts to bypass guardrails, extracting sensitive data, or degrading model performance. AiShield Guardian’s telemetry captures these events to enable detection and mitigation.
SOC Utilization: The SOC team leverages the enriched telemetry payloads to:
Detect Threats: Identify attack patterns using fields like LLM_AttackType, AdversarialIndicators, and Frameworks (e.g., OWASP Top 10 for LLMs, MITRE ATLAS).
Prioritize Response: Use Severity, PriorityScore, and ConfidenceScore to focus on high-risk events.
Investigate Incidents: Correlate events via CorrelationID, SourceIP, and SessionID to trace attack origins and scope.
Mitigate Risks: Apply Action_Taken insights (e.g., input blocking) and FeedbackAction to update policies or retrain models.
Ensure Compliance: Map events to frameworks like NIST AI RMF or OWASP for reporting and audits.
